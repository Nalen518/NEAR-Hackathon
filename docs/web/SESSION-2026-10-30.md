  - **02 The auction:** worker stats from `/v1/company`.
  - **03 The review:** every round, including a second opinion.
  - **04 The delivery:** locked until the payer signs in, then the ComplaintBox.
  - **05 What happened:** uses `lib/timeline.ts`.
- **Privacy (D-166):** the page is public by default. "Sign in to view" asks the wallet for a NEP-413 `signMessage` (`sharkco:view:<id>:<ts>`) and posts it to `/view`. The payer view lives only in that tab's memory (`gcTime: 0`, `cache: no-store`). Complaints sign `sharkco:complain:<id>:<ts>`.
- **Polling:** every 5 s until final, 60 s while Unresolved. "Can’t reach Shark" shows at once and retries every 5 s.
- **States:** every status from the states frame has a state card that says where the money is. Refunded and Unresolved jobs never show the delivery, even to the payer.
- **Mock:** remembers complaints, so a sent complaint moves the job to review.
